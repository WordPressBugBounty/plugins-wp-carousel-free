Editor-preview showcase images
==============================

Used only by the Marquee and Panorama editor previews (Pro feature showcase).
Never enqueued on the front end - see blocks/blocks/shared/edit/previewDemoItems.js.

Source
------

All eight photographs are food shots taken from the company's own WordPress
media library (uploads/2026/08). Each was centre-cropped to a target aspect
ratio, downscaled and re-encoded to WebP.

Files
-----

smooth photographs sit on the large canvases and the detail-heavy ones on the
small. Only the aspect ratio is meaningful to the layout engines.
